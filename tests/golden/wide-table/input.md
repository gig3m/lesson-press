# Plague stations

| Station | Plague | Reading | Action |
|---------|--------|---------|--------|
| One | Frogs covered the land. | Exodus 8:1-15 describes the second plague. | Hop around the room three times like a frog. |
| Two | Locusts ate the crops. | Exodus 10:1-20 narrates the locust swarm. | Pretend to nibble at imaginary leaves. |
| Three | Darkness covered Egypt. | Exodus 10:21-29 describes thick darkness. | Close your eyes and walk slowly. |
